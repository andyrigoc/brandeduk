Frequently Asked Questions

What is DTG printing?

Direct-to-Garment (DTG) printing applies a digital image directly onto the fabric using specialised inkjet technology, producing high-resolution, full-colour results without screens or stencils.

Is DTG good for small orders?

Yes, DTG requires minimal setup, which makes it particularly suitable for small batch orders or one-off custom designs.

Does DTG printing feel different to screen printing?

DTG inks are typically water-based and absorbed into the garment's fibres, resulting in a soft feel compared to some other printing methods.

What is the minimum order for DTG printing?

DTG follows our general minimum order of 8 garments, with a minimum order value of £150.

How long does DTG printing take?

Standard lead time is 7-10 working days following artwork approval and payment.
