Frequently Asked Questions

How many thread colours can you embroider?

Our embroidery machines can stitch designs using up to 6 thread colours, with no extra charge for using more colours.

Do you charge setup fees for embroidery?

Basic text designs are usually set up free of charge. Setup costs for logos depend on the size, stitch count and placement of the design.

How durable is embroidery compared to printing?

Embroidery is stitched directly into the fabric, so it lasts as long as the garment itself without losing quality or fading.

What is the minimum order for embroidery?

Our general minimum order is 8 garments, with a minimum order value of £150.

How long does embroidery take?

Standard lead time is 7-10 working days following artwork approval and payment.
