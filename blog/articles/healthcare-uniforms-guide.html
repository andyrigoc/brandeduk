Healthcare Uniforms Buying Guide

Choose healthcare uniforms that support movement, frequent washing and clear team identification.
By Branded UK Production Team · 22 September 2026 · Reviewed by Anderson Ricotta
IMAGE REQUIRED
healthcare-uniforms-buying-guide.webp

Choose healthcare uniforms by comparing tunics, polos, scrubs, fabric, fit, laundering, role colours and logo customisation.

Start with the care environment

Healthcare uniforms vary between clinics, dental practices, care homes, therapy businesses, reception teams and community staff. Begin with movement, contact, temperature and laundering requirements before selecting a style.

A reception polo and a clinical tunic may share the same colour and logo without serving the same function.

Check fabric and laundering

Confirm the wash instructions for the specific garment and compare them with the organisation’s laundry process. Frequent washing, drying and stain treatment can affect colour, fit and decoration. Do not assume every garment can be washed at the same temperature.

Breathability, stretch, pocket placement and opacity should be evaluated during movement.

Use colour to identify roles

Colour can help distinguish teams or responsibilities, but the system should remain simple. Too many colours create ordering problems and make replacement difficult. Record the approved colour for every role and avoid substituting a visually similar shade without approval.

The logo should contrast clearly and remain consistent across the colour range.

Choose suitable customisation

Compact embroidery can give healthcare uniforms a durable professional finish. Fine text and very lightweight fabrics need review. DTF printing may suit detailed logos on compatible garments, while names or roles can be added where the organisation requires them.

Personal information and role titles must be checked carefully before production.

Maintain an approved range

Keep a controlled list of tunics, polos, scrubs, fleeces or outer layers used by each role. Store sizes and allocations securely and update them when staff change.

This makes personalised clothing easier to reorder and avoids mismatched colours or logo positions across the team.
