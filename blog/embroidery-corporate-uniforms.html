Why Embroidery Looks More Professional for Corporate Uniforms

4 min read Embroidery / Corporate

The Corporate Advantage

For businesses in Kingston, Surbiton and South West London, embroidery delivers a premium, professional finish that printing cannot match for corporate settings.

Why Embroidery Wins for Corporate

  • Tactile quality — raised thread adds dimension and prestige
  • Durability — withstands industrial washing cycles
  • Professional perception — clients associate embroidery with quality
  • Longevity — will not fade, crack or peel over time

Best Applications

  • Polo shirts for client-facing staff
  • Oxford shirts for office teams
  • Jackets and outerwear
  • Fleeces for casual professional settings

Preparation Tips

Ensure your logo is in vector format. Consider washing frequency and visibility requirements when choosing placement.
