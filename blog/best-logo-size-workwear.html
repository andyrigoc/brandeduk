Best Logo Size for Workwear: Chest, Back & Sleeves Explained

4 min read Guide / Workwear

Getting Logo Size Right

For businesses in Kingston, Surbiton and South West London, choosing the right logo size impacts cost, durability and brand perception across your workwear.

Recommended Sizes by Position

  • Left chest — 8-10cm wide (standard placement, most professional)
  • Back print — 25-30cm wide (maximum visibility on-site)
  • Sleeve — 6-8cm wide (subtle, secondary branding)

Key Considerations

  • Garment type — polo shirts vs hoodies require different sizing
  • Logo complexity — detailed logos need minimum size for clarity
  • Method — embroidery has stitch-count implications for larger logos

Before Production

Ensure your logo is in vector format to avoid delays and extra setup costs. Always consider visibility requirements when choosing placement and size.
