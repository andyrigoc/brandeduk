Common Mistakes When Ordering Branded Clothing

4 min read Guide / Tips

Errors That Cost You Money

For businesses in Kingston, Surbiton and South West London, understanding common ordering mistakes saves time and money on every workwear order.

Top Mistakes to Avoid

  • Wrong file format — sending JPG/PNG instead of vector files causes delays
  • Inconsistent sizing — changing logo size between orders breaks brand consistency
  • Cheap garments — low-quality base garments shrink, fade, and need replacing
  • Wrong method — using printing where embroidery is needed (or vice versa)
  • No sample — skipping proof approval leads to unwanted results
  • Rush ordering — last-minute orders cost more and risk quality

How to Get It Right

  • Always provide vector format logos (.AI, .EPS, .SVG)
  • Standardise logo size and placement from the start
  • Choose garments based on usage — not just price
  • Request a proof or sample before bulk production

Working with an experienced supplier ensures correct placement, sizing, and execution — reducing long-term costs and improving brand consistency.
