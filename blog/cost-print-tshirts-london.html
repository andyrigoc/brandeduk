Understanding T-Shirt Printing Costs

For businesses in Kingston, Surbiton and South West London, choosing the right printing approach impacts cost, durability and brand perception.

What Affects the Price?

  • Garment type — quality of the base t-shirt
  • Logo complexity — number of colours and detail level
  • Order quantity — bulk orders reduce cost per unit significantly
  • Printing method — screen print, DTF, or DTG each have different cost structures

Cost-Saving Tips

  • Ensure your logo is in vector format to avoid delays and extra setup costs
  • Order in bulk to benefit from volume pricing
  • Choose the right method for your quantity — screen print for large runs, DTF for smaller batches

Durability Considerations

Always consider washing frequency, environment, and visibility requirements when choosing your method. Working with an experienced supplier ensures correct placement, sizing, and execution.

A structured approach reduces long-term costs and improves overall brand consistency.
