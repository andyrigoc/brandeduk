DTF vs Screen Printing: Which Is Better for Bulk Orders?

4 min read Printing / Technical

Two Leading Methods Compared

For businesses in Kingston, Surbiton and South West London, choosing between DTF and screen printing impacts cost, durability and brand perception.

Screen Printing

  • Best for large volume runs (100+ units)
  • Lower cost per unit at scale
  • Excellent colour vibrancy and durability
  • Setup costs make small runs expensive

DTF (Direct-to-Film) Printing

  • Best for smaller to medium runs
  • No setup costs — print-on-demand capability
  • Supports full-colour, photo-quality designs
  • Slightly higher per-unit cost for very large runs

Which Should You Choose?

The correct decision depends on garment type, logo complexity, and order quantity. A balanced approach often combines different techniques depending on the use case.

Before production, ensure your logo is in vector format to avoid delays and extra setup costs.
