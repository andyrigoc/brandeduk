Case Study: 500 T-Shirts Printed for a London Event

4 min read Case Study

The Brief

A London-based events company needed 500 custom printed t-shirts for a major community event in South West London. The requirement: bold, colourful prints on a mix of sizes — from kids' to adults' — delivered within 10 working days.

Order summary: 500 t-shirts / 4 colours / 6 sizes / Front + back print / 10-day turnaround

The Challenge

  • Tight timeline — only 10 working days from artwork approval to delivery
  • Size range — from age 5–6 kids' sizes through to 5XL adults
  • Multi-location print — full colour front design plus sponsor logos on the back
  • Budget constraints — needed to stay within a strict per-unit cost

Our Approach

1. Garment Selection

We recommended the Gildan Heavy Cotton tee — a proven, cost-effective option that handles bold prints well and is available across the full size range. Available in the client's preferred colour (Royal Blue) from stock.

2. Print Method

For a 500-unit order with full colour artwork, screen printing was the clear choice. Lower per-unit cost at volume, vibrant colour reproduction, and fast production speeds made it the ideal method.

3. Artwork Preparation

The client provided their front design as a PDF and sponsor logos in mixed formats. Our team:

  • Converted all files to print-ready separations
  • Standardised Pantone colour references across all logos
  • Provided a digital proof within 24 hours for approval

4. Production

  • Day 1–2: Artwork preparation and proof approval
  • Day 3: Screen creation (4 screens for front, 2 for back)
  • Day 4–7: Printing across all sizes
  • Day 8: Quality check and packing by size
  • Day 9–10: Delivery to venue

The Result

  • ✅ 500 t-shirts delivered on time
  • ✅ Consistent print quality across all sizes
  • ✅ Under budget — bulk pricing kept costs below target
  • ✅ Client reordered 200 more for a follow-up event

Client feedback: "The shirts looked amazing. Everyone wanted one. We'll definitely use BrandedUK again for our next event."

Key Takeaways for Event Orders

  • Order early — even with fast turnaround, 2–3 weeks lead time is ideal
  • Choose the right method — screen printing wins on cost and speed for 100+ units
  • Provide vector artwork — this avoids delays in the proofing stage
  • Confirm sizes upfront — get a size breakdown before ordering to avoid surplus
  • Order 5–10% extra — spare shirts cover last-minute additions and replacements
