Hospitality & Restaurant Uniforms in Surbiton and Kingston

5 min read Hospitality

Why Uniforms Matter in Hospitality

Customers judge instantly. Staff appearance directly impacts perception of quality, hygiene, and professionalism.

Recommended Setup

  • Embroidered polos or shirts — front-of-house
  • Branded aprons — printed or embroidered
  • Lightweight t-shirts — kitchen staff

Branding Strategy

  • Embroidery → front-of-house (premium look)
  • Printing → aprons and bulk items (cost-effective)

Key Requirements

  • Easy to wash
  • Durable fabrics
  • Breathable materials

Common Mistakes

  • Cheap garments that shrink
  • Inconsistent branding across staff
  • Overcomplicated logos on thin fabrics

Operational Tip: Keep spare uniforms ready to replace damaged items immediately — critical in hospitality.
