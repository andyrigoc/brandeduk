Hi-Vis Workwear in South West London: Compliance Meets Branding

5 min read Hi-Vis / Safety

More Than Just Safety Gear

Hi-vis clothing is mandatory across many industries — but it also acts as mobile advertising for your company. Most businesses ignore this.

Compliance Requirements

Your hi-vis garments should include:

  • High visibility colour (yellow or orange)
  • Reflective strips
  • Certified safety standards

How to Brand Hi-Vis Correctly

  • Small chest logo — clean and readable
  • Large back print — maximum visibility

Avoid overcomplicated designs. Keep it bold and simple.

Best Printing Methods

For hi-vis garments:

  • Screen printing → best for bulk orders
  • DTF printing → flexible for smaller runs

Operational Advantage

Branded hi-vis:

  • Makes your team instantly recognisable
  • Improves professionalism on-site
  • Reinforces brand presence daily
