Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order for custom polo shirts?

Our general minimum order is 8 garments, with a minimum order value of £150. Screen printing has a separate minimum of 25 garments.

Can I get my logo embroidered on a polo shirt?

Yes, embroidery is our most popular choice for polo shirts, using up to 6 thread colours at no extra cost for most designs.

How long does it take to receive custom polo shirts?

Standard lead time is 7-10 working days following artwork approval and payment.

Do you offer printed polo shirts as well as embroidered?

Yes, we offer screen printing and DTF printing on polo shirts as well as embroidery, depending on your design and order size.

Do prices include VAT?

Prices are quoted +VAT unless stated otherwise.
