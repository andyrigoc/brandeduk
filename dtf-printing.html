Frequently Asked Questions

What is DTF printing?

DTF (heat transfer) printing applies a design to transfer film, then presses it onto the garment using heat and pressure, allowing full-colour images without the need for screens.

Is there a minimum order for DTF printing?

DTF follows our general minimum order of 8 garments, with no additional per-colour minimums, making it suitable for smaller or mixed-size orders.

Can you add names and numbers with DTF?

Yes, DTF is well suited to adding individual names, numbers and initials without extra setup fees.

How durable is DTF printing?

Durability depends on care. We recommend washing at 40°C, inside out, and avoiding ironing directly on the print.

How long does DTF printing take?

Standard lead time is 7-10 working days following artwork approval and payment.
