Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order for custom workwear?

Our general minimum order is 8 garments, with a minimum order value of £150. Screen printing has a separate minimum of 25 garments, and custom caps start from 16.

Which customisation method is best for workwear?

Embroidery is the most popular choice for durability on daily-wear workwear, while screen printing suits larger bulk orders and DTF/DTG suit smaller or detailed designs.

How long does production take?

Standard lead time is 7-10 working days following artwork approval and payment.

Do you supply workwear for specific industries?

Yes, we supply hospitality, construction, corporate, school, sports and outdoor teams, each with garments suited to their needs.

Do prices include VAT?

Prices are quoted +VAT unless stated otherwise.
