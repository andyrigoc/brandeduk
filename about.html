About Branded UK

Branded UK is the trading name of Branded Europe Limited, a custom workwear, uniform and embroidery specialist based in Surbiton, Surrey, serving businesses across London and Surrey since 2001.

Who We Are

Branded UK has traded under that name since 2001, supplying customised uniforms, embroidered workwear and printed garments to businesses, schools, hospitality venues, construction companies and sports clubs. We are a specialist supplier, not a large chain - our focus is getting your logo onto the right garment correctly, every time.

What We Do

We offer commercial embroidery, screen printing, DTF printing and DTG printing, and can advise on the right decoration method for your garments, quantity and budget. Our range covers T-shirts, polo shirts, hoodies, jackets, hi-vis workwear, headwear and more.

Our Services Hospitality Workwear Construction Workwear Corporate Uniforms

Who We Work With

We supply businesses and organisations of all sizes, including hospitality venues and cafés, construction and trade companies, corporate offices, schools and sports clubs across London, Surrey and the wider UK.

Where We're Based

Branded UK is based at Unit 8, Red Lion Business Centre, Red Lion Road, Surbiton, Surrey, KT6 7QD. We serve customers across London, Greater London, Surrey and Kingston upon Thames, as well as the rest of the UK.

How We Work

Choose your products, send us your logo, and we'll confirm positions and pricing before anything goes into production. Standard lead time is 7-10 working days following artwork approval and payment. Our general minimum order is 8 garments (minimum order value £150); screen printing has a separate minimum of 25 garments.
