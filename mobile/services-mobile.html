Embroidery

Embroidery decorates fabric by stitching thread directly into the material to create designs, logos or text. It is renowned for its durability and professional appearance, making it ideal for corporate clothing, uniforms and promotional items. Modern embroidery machines produce intricate, multi-colour designs with consistent, high-quality results.

  • Durability

    5 out of 5.

    Embroidery will last as long as the garment itself without losing quality.

  • Colours

    5 out of 5.

    The number of colours used does not affect the price. Machines can handle up to 6 colours.

  • Setup costs

    5 out of 5.

    Logo setup is free on orders over £300. Below that, setup costs depend on the size and placement of the embroidery.

  • Complexity

    4 out of 5.

    Can accommodate most designs but not ideal for photographic details, gradients or spray effects.

  • Personalised

    5 out of 5.

    Initials and names can be added without setup fees for team orders.

  • Cost

    4 out of 5.

    Cost is determined by the size and stitch count of the embroidery. Larger, more detailed embroidery costs more.

Screen Printing

Screen printing applies ink to a garment through a mesh screen prepared with a stencil of your design. Each colour needs its own screen, and the ink is pushed through the mesh with a squeegee. It produces vibrant, long-lasting prints and is particularly cost-effective for large orders once the screens are made.

  • Durability

    5 out of 5.

    Print will maintain its quality for the garment's lifespan and fade naturally over time.

  • Colours

    4 out of 5.

    We can print up to 6 colours using both manual and automatic printing methods.

  • Setup costs

    5 out of 5.

    Setup fees apply per colour and per design. For example, two designs with two colours = four setups.

  • Complexity

    3 out of 5.

    Handles most design needs, but small details are limited. No gradients or high-detail patterns.

  • Personalised

    2 out of 5.

    Not the best choice for individual customisation; better suited to bulk production.

  • Cost

    4 out of 5.

    Cost is based on the number of garments, colours and printing positions.

Heat Transfer Printing

Heat transfer printing moves a design from special transfer paper onto the garment using heat and pressure. The design is printed onto the transfer paper first and then applied with a heat press. It allows detailed, full-colour images on fabrics and other materials, and is popular for custom clothing and promotional items thanks to its versatility and high-quality results.

  • Durability

    3 out of 5.

    Print durability depends on washing. We recommend washing at 40°C, inside out, and avoiding ironing.

  • Colours

    5 out of 5.

    Various vinyl colours are available, but specific Pantone shades may need DTG or screen printing.

  • Setup costs

    5 out of 5.

    No setup fees for basic designs. A wide variety of designs are accepted.

  • Complexity

    3 out of 5.

    Best for simple shapes, text, names and logos. Complex details may not work well.

  • Personalised

    5 out of 5.

    An ideal process for adding personal touches (names, initials, multi-design options).

  • Cost

    4 out of 5.

    Price depends on the size of the design because of the material used.

Direct-to-Garment (DTG) Printing

Direct-to-garment (DTG) printing prints a digital image straight onto the fabric using specialised inkjet technology. It produces high-resolution, full-colour designs with intricate detail, without screens or stencils. Because setup is minimal, DTG suits small batches and one-off designs. The water-based inks are absorbed by the fibres, giving a soft feel and a durable print.

  • Durability

    4 out of 5.

    The life of the print depends on how the garment is washed. Wash at 40°C, turn inside out and avoid ironing for best results.

  • Colours

    5 out of 5.

    Suitable for photographic-quality prints, gradients and high-detail designs, with a high level of customisation.

  • Setup costs

    5 out of 5.

    No setup fees for designs. We welcome a wide range of creative ideas.

  • Complexity

    5 out of 5.

    Handles fine details and complex designs with ease.

  • Personalised

    5 out of 5.

    A highly adaptable option for customising clothing (multiple designs, initials, names and colour combinations).

  • Cost

    3 out of 5.

    Pricing depends on the size of the print because of the materials used. The number of colours does not affect the price.

Fleet Livery & Wraps

Fleet livery and wraps apply large vinyl graphics or decals to vehicles, turning them into mobile advertisements. A wrap can cover part or all of a vehicle and is customised with your branding, including logos, contact details and promotional messages. Wraps are a cost-effective way to reach a broad audience as your vehicles travel, and they protect the original paintwork and can be removed or updated as needed.

Digital Printing

Digital printing prints digital images directly onto a wide range of media. Unlike traditional methods it needs no printing plates, so it suits short runs and on-demand printing. It offers quick turnaround and easy customisation, making it ideal for personalised marketing materials, business cards and custom textiles, with sharp, vibrant results comparable to traditional printing.
