Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, store and protect personal data during and after your relationship with us, including the rights available to you under UK data protection law.

Last updated · 30 September 2026 · Download PDF

1. Introduction

We value the trust you place in us when you provide personal information. Protecting that information is important to Branded Europe Limited.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect personal data during and after your relationship with us. It also outlines when information may be shared, the lawful grounds on which we process it, and the rights available to you under applicable data protection law.

2. Who we are and the scope of this policy

For the purposes of the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018, Branded Europe Limited, trading as Branded UK, is the Data Controller for the personal information covered by this policy. We decide what personal data is collected, why it is used and how it is protected.

This policy applies to personal data collected through our website, by telephone, email, live chat, social media, in person, and through other business interactions connected with our products and services.

Please read this policy together with any additional privacy information we may provide when collecting or processing personal data for a particular purpose.

If you have questions about how we handle personal information or wish to exercise any of your rights, please use the contact details in section 14.

3. The kinds of personal data we may hold

We may collect, store and use categories of personal information including, but not limited to:

  • Personal contact information, such as your name, title, postal address, telephone number and personal email address.
  • Business contact information, including job title, company name, business address, telephone number and business email address.
  • Age or date of birth where this is relevant to the service, transaction or legal requirement.
  • Bank account information and tax or VAT status details where required for payment, accounting or commercial administration.
  • Trading, account or credit information where credit facilities or payment terms are requested or provided.
  • CCTV footage where applicable, and delivery-related information such as delivery location or other electronic information used to fulfil an order.
  • Cookie data and information about how you use our website, communications and online services.

4. How and why we collect personal data

We may process personal information for one or more of the following purposes:

  • To perform a contract with you, or with the company or organisation you represent. In some cases, your organisation may provide your details to us.
  • To begin, manage and complete commercial transactions for the purchase or supply of products and services.
  • To manufacture, customise, process, dispatch and deliver products ordered from us.
  • To communicate with customers, suppliers, contractors and service providers.
  • To maintain accounts, invoices, transaction records and other business documentation.
  • To carry out appropriate marketing and business communications, subject to applicable consent and direct-marketing rules.
  • To comply with legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, health and safety, or other statutory obligations.
  • To respond to enquiries, complaints, disputes and customer-service requests.
  • To generate analytics that help us understand and improve our website, services and commercial performance.
  • To protect the security, reliability and proper operation of our website, IT systems and business infrastructure.
  • To obtain relevant information from third parties, which may include credit reference or identity-verification providers where appropriate.

Technical and cookie information

To help our website operate effectively and to understand how visitors use it, we may collect technical information such as:

  • The Internet Protocol (IP) address used by your device to connect to the internet.
  • Login information, browser type and version, cookie identifiers, time-zone settings, and browser plug-in types and versions.
  • Operating system, device type and platform information.
  • Information about your visit, including pages viewed and the URL clickstream to, through and from our website.

5. Lawful bases for processing personal data

We process personal data only where we have a lawful basis to do so. Depending on the circumstances, processing may be necessary to perform a contract, to comply with a legal obligation, for our legitimate business interests, or because you have given consent.

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we consider whether those interests are balanced against your rights, freedoms and reasonable expectations. In some circumstances we may also need to use or disclose information to comply with a lawful request from a public authority or regulator.

6. Sharing your personal data

We may share personal information on a limited, need-to-know basis where this is necessary for our business operations, to provide a service, or to comply with a legal requirement. Recipients may include:

  • Accountants and financial advisers.
  • Legal advisers and professional consultants.
  • Insurers.
  • IT, hosting, software and communications providers.
  • Garment, product, production and other commercial suppliers.
  • Couriers, logistics providers and transport services.

We may also disclose personal data to competent law-enforcement bodies, courts, regulators or other authorities where required by law, and in connection with a sale, acquisition, restructuring or transfer of all or part of our business or assets.

Where third parties process personal information on our behalf, we take reasonable steps to require appropriate confidentiality, data protection and security measures.

7. International transfers

Some suppliers, technology providers or other service providers we use may process personal information outside the United Kingdom. When a transfer is subject to the UK GDPR rules on restricted international transfers, we take steps designed to ensure that the information receives an appropriate level of protection.

Where applicable, this may include relying on UK adequacy regulations, using appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) or the UK Addendum to approved standard contractual clauses, carrying out any required transfer risk assessment, or relying on another lawful transfer mechanism permitted by data protection law.

We also expect third-party service providers handling personal information on our behalf to apply suitable technical and organisational security measures and to process personal data in accordance with applicable law.

8. How long we keep personal data

We keep personal information only for as long as it is reasonably required for the purpose for which it was collected, for legitimate business needs, and to meet legal, accounting, tax, regulatory or contractual retention requirements. Personal data remains subject to appropriate security measures while it is retained.

When deciding how long information should be kept, we consider the amount, nature and sensitivity of the data, the potential risk of harm from unauthorised use or disclosure, the reason we process it, whether the purpose can be achieved in another way, and any applicable legal requirements.

9. Security measures

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures intended to reduce the risk of personal data being accidentally lost, misused, accessed without authorisation, altered or disclosed. Access to personal information is restricted to staff, contractors, agents and service providers who have a legitimate business need to use it.

No internet transmission or information system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for taking reasonable precautions to protect your own devices, passwords and communications. We cannot control security risks that arise outside the boundary of systems and services for which we are responsible.

10. Your rights as a data subject

Subject to applicable law and any relevant exemptions, you may have the following rights in relation to personal data we hold about you. We may request reasonable information to verify your identity before acting on a request.

The right to be informed: You have the right to clear and transparent information about how your personal data is collected and used. This policy is part of that information.

The right of access: You may request confirmation that we process your personal data and ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, together with relevant supplementary information.

The right to rectification: You may ask us to correct personal data that is inaccurate and to complete information that is incomplete.

The right to erasure: In certain circumstances, you may ask us to delete personal data where there is no continuing lawful reason for us to retain or process it.

The right to restrict processing: In certain circumstances, you may ask us to limit the way we use your personal data while an issue is being considered.

The right to data portability: Where the legal conditions are met, you may request personal data that you provided to us in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format, or ask for it to be transferred to another controller where technically feasible.

The right to object: You may object to certain processing carried out on the basis of legitimate interests or in the public interest, and you may object at any time to processing for direct marketing.

The right to withdraw consent: Where processing relies on your consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before consent was withdrawn.

Rights relating to automated decision-making: Where applicable law gives you rights in relation to decisions made solely by automated means that produce legal or similarly significant effects, you may be entitled to obtain information about the processing and request human involvement or challenge the decision.

There is normally no fee for exercising your data protection rights. We will respond without undue delay and, where the UK GDPR one-month time limit applies, within one month unless the law permits an extension.

11. Do you have to provide personal data to us?

You are not generally required to provide personal information to us. However, certain information may be necessary when you enter into a contract with us, place an order, request a quotation, ask us to deliver goods or services, apply for credit terms, or exercise a data protection right.

If you do not provide information that is necessary for a particular transaction or legal requirement, we may be unable to provide the relevant product or service or complete your request.

12. Automated processes and cookies

We may use automated processes to help personalise or present website content based on information collected through cookies or similar technologies. Where consent is required for non-essential cookies, we will request it through our cookie consent mechanism.

You can manage your cookie choices using the cookie preference controls available on our website.

13. Marketing and cookie consent management

We may use personal data for marketing and business-development communications where this is permitted by law. Where consent is required, we will rely on that consent. In other circumstances, we may rely on legitimate interests where appropriate. You may object to or opt out of direct marketing at any time. This will not prevent us from sending service, transactional or administrative communications that are necessary in connection with an order, account or enquiry.

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by websites you visit. We use cookies and similar technologies to support essential website functions, understand website usage, remember preferences and, where permitted, provide content that is more relevant to visitors.

You can also control or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking all cookies may prevent certain areas or functions of our website from operating correctly.
