1. Introduction

At BrandedUK, we are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly, securely and transparently.

This Privacy & Cookies Policy explains how Branded Europe Limited, trading as BrandedUK, collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you visit our website, create an account, request a quotation, purchase products or communicate with our team.

We process personal data in accordance with applicable UK data protection legislation, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018 and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR), as amended.

By using our website, you acknowledge that you have been provided with this policy. Where your consent is legally required, we will request it separately.

3. What Personal Information We Collect

We collect only information that is reasonably necessary for operating our business, providing our products and services, maintaining customer relationships and fulfilling our legal obligations.

3.1 Information you provide directly

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Full name, company name and job title.
  • Email address, telephone number and business contact details.
  • Billing, delivery and correspondence addresses.
  • Account registration and login information.
  • Order history, product selections, sizes, quantities and customisation preferences.
  • Artwork, company logos, embroidery designs, printing instructions and other files uploaded to our website.
  • Quotation requests, purchase orders and transaction information.
  • Customer service enquiries, emails, WhatsApp messages and web chat communications.
  • Feedback, reviews and information voluntarily provided through surveys.
  • Marketing preferences and newsletter subscriptions.

Uploaded artwork may contain personal information, such as employee names, photographs or other identifiable details.

3.2 Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, including:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device type and operating system.
  • Website pages visited and navigation activity.
  • Referral sources and search terms.
  • Date, time and duration of visits.
  • Shopping basket and account session information.
  • Cookie identifiers and website preferences.
  • Technical error logs and security-related information.

This information helps us operate the website, maintain security, understand website performance and improve the customer experience.

3.3 Information from third parties

We may receive limited personal information from:

  • Payment processing providers.
  • Delivery and logistics companies.
  • Business representatives placing orders on behalf of their organisations.
  • Suppliers and professional service providers.
  • Advertising and analytics platforms, where permitted.
  • Publicly available business directories and professional websites, where we have a lawful basis to use the information.

We process information received from third parties in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

4. How We Use Your Personal Information

We use personal information for legitimate business purposes, including:

Processing orders and quotations

To prepare quotations, process purchases, confirm orders, arrange production, manage payments and deliver customised garments or other products.

Managing customer accounts

To create and maintain customer accounts, save relevant order information, display previous purchases and facilitate repeat orders.

Artwork and customisation

To prepare, digitise, process, print or embroider customer-supplied artwork and logos onto selected products.

Where appropriate, we may retain approved artwork and production specifications to make future orders more convenient.

Customer support

To respond to enquiries, provide assistance, handle complaints and communicate about quotations, orders, deliveries and after-sales services.

Business administration

To maintain accounting records, issue invoices, manage outstanding payments, prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations.

Website improvement

To monitor performance, identify technical problems, analyse relevant usage information and improve the functionality and usability of our website.

Marketing and communications

To provide relevant information about our products, services, offers and business opportunities where permitted by applicable law.

Security and fraud prevention

To protect our systems, customer accounts, transactions and business operations against unauthorised access, misuse or fraudulent activity.

6. Online Orders and Payment Security

When you place an order through BrandedUK.com, we collect the information necessary to process your purchase and fulfil our contractual obligations.

Payments are handled through secure third-party payment service providers, including Stripe where available at checkout.

Payment providers may collect and process information such as:

  • Cardholder name.
  • Billing address.
  • Payment method and payment details.
  • Transaction amount and currency.
  • Payment verification and fraud-prevention information.

BrandedUK does not routinely receive or store your full payment card number or card security code.

Payment providers may process your information under their own privacy policies and security procedures.

Some checkout options may offer accelerated payment or saved payment details through services operated by the payment provider. These features are subject to the provider's own terms, privacy settings and applicable consent requirements.

For more information about Stripe's processing of personal information, please visit https://stripe.com/privacy.

14. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to support essential functions, remember preferences, improve performance and, where permitted, analyse usage or support advertising.

A cookie is a small data file stored on your device when you visit a website.

We may also use related technologies such as browser storage, tracking pixels and website scripts.

14.1 Types of cookies

Strictly necessary cookies

These support essential website operations, such as:

  • Shopping basket functionality.
  • Account authentication and login sessions.
  • Checkout and payment security.
  • Fraud prevention.
  • Website security and session management.
  • Remembering privacy and cookie preferences.

These technologies may be used without consent where a relevant legal exception applies.

Functional and preference cookies

These may remember selected preferences, such as display settings or other website customisations.

Some functional technologies may qualify for a legal exception, while others require consent.

Analytics and performance cookies

These help us understand website traffic and visitor interactions so we can improve the performance, content and usability of our website.

Where analytics technologies meet the conditions of a statutory statistical-purpose exception, they may be used without prior consent, provided the required information and a simple, free means to object are supplied.

Analytics technologies that do not qualify for an exception will require consent.

With your consent, we also use Microsoft Clarity, provided by Microsoft Corporation, to understand how visitors use our website through heatmaps and session recordings of clicks, scrolling and mouse movements; form entries are masked, and Clarity cookies (such as _clck and _clsk) are set only after you accept analytics cookies. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement at https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement.

Our website live chat is provided by Crisp IM SAS. The chat only loads after you accept analytics cookies; it then sets cookies (such as crisp-client/session) so that your conversation stays connected while you browse, and the messages you send are stored in Crisp so that our team can reply. See Crisp's privacy policy at https://crisp.chat/en/privacy/.

Marketing and advertising cookies

These may be used, where enabled, to:

  • Measure advertising campaign performance.
  • Understand how visitors interact with advertisements.
  • Support relevant advertising and remarketing.
  • Measure website conversions.

Marketing and advertising tracking technologies requiring consent will not be activated before valid consent is obtained.

14.2 Your cookie choices

Where required, our cookie preference controls allow you to:

  • Accept optional cookies.
  • Reject optional cookies.
  • Customise cookie categories.
  • Change or withdraw previous consent.
  • Object to eligible consent-exempt statistical or preference technologies.

Strictly necessary technologies cannot always be disabled through website controls because they are required for essential functionality.

Where the law requires consent, rejecting optional cookies will not prevent you from accessing the core functionality of our website.

Open cookie settings

14.3 Cookie details and retention

Individual cookies and similar technologies may vary depending on the website features and third-party services in use.

The applicable cookie information should identify each technology's name, provider, purpose and retention period through the website's cookie settings or cookie declaration.

14.4 Browser settings

Most browsers allow you to manage or delete cookies.

You can generally block cookies or remove existing cookies through your browser's privacy settings.

Please note that disabling certain essential cookies may affect your ability to log in, maintain a shopping basket or complete checkout.

For general information about cookies, visit https://www.allaboutcookies.org.
