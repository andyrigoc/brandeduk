Frequently Asked Questions

Do you make branded aprons for cafés and coffee shops?

Yes. We supply embroidered and printed aprons, bib aprons and waist aprons for cafés, coffee shops and restaurants, along with polo shirts, T-shirts and chef wear with your logo.

What is the minimum order for hospitality uniforms?

Our general minimum order is 8 garments, with a minimum order value of £150. Screen printing has a separate minimum of 25 garments, and custom caps start from 16.

How long does production take?

Standard lead time is 7-10 working days following artwork approval and payment.

Can I supply my own aprons or garments for branding?

We normally source the garments as part of the order rather than branding customer-supplied items, so we can guarantee quality and consistent sizing across your team.

Do prices include VAT?

Prices are quoted +VAT unless stated otherwise.
