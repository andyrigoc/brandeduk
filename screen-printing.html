Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order for screen printing?

Screen printing has a minimum order of 25 garments, as each design and colour requires its own screen to be prepared.

How many colours can you print?

We can print up to 6 colours using manual and automatic screen printing presses.

Why is screen printing cost-effective for large orders?

Once the screens are prepared, the printing process is fast and efficient, which lowers the per-garment cost on larger quantities.

How durable is screen printing?

Screen printed designs maintain their quality for the garment's lifespan and fade naturally over time with normal washing.

How long does screen printing take?

Standard lead time is 7-10 working days following artwork approval and payment.
