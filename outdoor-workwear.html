Frequently Asked Questions

Can you embroider logos on softshell jackets and fleeces?

Yes, embroidery is a popular choice for softshell jackets, fleeces and body warmers, giving a durable, professional finish for outdoor wear.

What is the minimum order for outdoor workwear?

Embroidery has a minimum of 8 pieces, and printed orders have a minimum order value of £50 + VAT (goods only, delivery not included).

Is embroidery durable enough for outdoor use?

Yes, embroidery is stitched directly into the fabric, so it withstands regular outdoor wear and washing without losing quality.

How long does production take?

Standard lead time is 7-10 working days following artwork approval and payment.

Do prices include VAT?

Prices are quoted +VAT unless stated otherwise.
